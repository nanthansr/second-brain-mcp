# Spaced repetition

Reviewing material at increasing intervals right before you would forget it. The
technique behind flashcard apps like Anki.

## How Alex uses it

- 20 minutes daily for Spanish vocabulary, streak of 94 days
- Cards are sentence-first, never isolated words
- New cards capped at 15/day to keep reviews under control

## Observed results

- B1 reading comprehension reached in ~4 months
- Speaking lags behind - flashcards do not train production, tutoring does
