# Kite Weather App

Fictional project. Hyperlocal wind forecasting for kitesurfers: fuses two public
forecast models with beach-level crowdsourced readings, then pushes a "go / no-go"
notification two hours before the session window.

## Status (2026-08-19)

- 34 beta testers across 3 Lisbon-area spots
- Day-7 retention 38%; the notification is what retains, the map is ignored
- Onboarding redesigned by [[sam-okafor]]: activation 40% -> 71%

## Open decisions

- Pricing: one-time purchase vs 4 EUR/month subscription
- Whether to expand to Ericeira before or after monetization
